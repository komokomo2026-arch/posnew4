Who we are

EKAMBANI System brings practical technology to businesses and individuals in one application. The idea is simple: keep the everyday tools you need for records, sales, finances and repairs together, easy to use, and available on the device you already have.

What is inside

  • EKAMBANI POS – record sales and purchases, manage products and stock, print invoices, scan barcodes, track supplies, and see reports and sales analysis.
  • Personal Finance – record income and expenses for yourself and your businesses and produce clear statements.
  • Phone Repair – register repair jobs, print invoices, follow payments and keep a searchable history of customers.
  • More – an additional EKAMBANI web service, opened inside the same app.

Why one application

Many small businesses use several separate tools. EKAMBANI puts them side by side while keeping each module's records separate, so sales, personal finances and repair jobs never get mixed. Each module has its own backup, so you stay in control of your information.

Business and record management

Your records are kept on your own device and keep working without internet. Optional Google Drive backups help you protect them and move to a new device.
