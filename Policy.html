Privacy Policy

How EKAMBANI System handles information
‹ EKAMBANI

This policy describes what the application actually does. The app has no advertising or analytics tools of its own, and it does not send your sales, finance or repair records to an EKAMBANI server.

1. Information you enter

  • POS: products, prices, stock, purchases, supply records, sale details you choose to enter, and business settings.
  • Personal Finance: businesses, income and expense records and descriptions.
  • Phone Repair: customer names and phone numbers, device details, repair descriptions, payments, invoice numbers and optional signatures.

2. Where it is stored

These records are stored in your browser's local database (IndexedDB) on your own device, and work offline. Each module uses its own database. Anyone who can use your device and browser can see these records. The app does not encrypt the local database; use your device's own security such as a screen lock.

3. Google Drive backup and restore

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks Google for permission to create and manage only the files and folders the app itself creates in your Drive. Backup files are written to folders you name (separate folders for POS, Finance and Phone Repair). Google processes this under its own terms and privacy policy. Backup files are ordinary files in your Drive and are not encrypted by the app. The app keeps a short-lived access token only in memory, and shares only your Google account e-mail between modules on the same device so you need not choose the account again.

4. Subscription information

The subscription system uses a Google Apps Script service and a Google Sheet. It stores your Device ID (a random identifier created by the app), the subscription start and end dates, and whether a renewal code has been used. It does not receive your sales, finance or repair records.

5. The More module

More opens an EKAMBANI web service hosted on Google Apps Script. What it collects is determined by that service and is outside the local records described above.

6. Third-party services used

  • Google: sign-in and Drive for backups; Apps Script and Sheets for subscriptions.
  • Content delivery services for page resources such as icons, fonts and libraries (for example cdnjs, jsDelivr and Google Fonts). When these load, the provider can see your IP address and browser details, as with any website.
  • WhatsApp, only if you tap a contact button; messages you send there are shared with us.

7. Your responsibility and control

You decide what to enter, when to back up and whether to connect Google Drive. You are responsible for keeping backups. You can delete records inside each module, remove backup files from your own Drive, and withdraw Google's permission in your Google account. Clearing your browser's site data permanently deletes the local records, so back up first.

8. Deleting and restoring

Deleting a record removes it from the local database. A restore adds records from your own backup; the Phone Repair and Finance restores keep records already on the device rather than overwriting them.

9. Offline use

After the app has loaded once, the POS, Finance and Phone Repair work without internet. Subscription renewal, Google Drive and the More module need a connection.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the app changes. The version on this page is the current one.
